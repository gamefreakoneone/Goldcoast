# Requirements: business and brand onboarding

Create tenant-scoped food and drink business profiles and a private visual brand library. Owners enter factual name, city, timezone, products, hours, website and optional verified offers. Prompt engineering is not required. Accept logo, product photo, reference ad/image, PDF guidelines, and font files with size/type validation. Brand analysis reads uploaded images and guidelines and returns an editable draft kit with palette, voice, layout, prohibited content and source asset IDs. Inferred preferences remain unconfirmed until explicitly saved by the owner. A ready kit references owned assets only. Material edits increment versions for later campaign invalidation. No provider calls occur during upload; analysis requires a separately reserved brand job. Bound uploads and decoded image dimensions. Serve private media only after tenant-scoped lookup.
