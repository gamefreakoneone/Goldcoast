# Requirements: branded creative production

Generate one landscape 1920x1080 and one portrait 1080x1920 ad for the selected campaign, using the confirmed visual references, product photos and brand rules. Preserve logos and exact text with controlled HTML/CSS composition rendered in headless Chromium. AI generates imagery and proposes a typed creative brief. Brief products and offers must match owner-confirmed facts. Judge the finished composite for factual accuracy, brand fidelity, visual quality and legibility before it is available for approval. At most two retries per format and six image calls per campaign. No unbounded regeneration. Persist every judged attempt and its verdict. Humans approve or reject; only approved passing current ads export. Private media and decisions are tenant scoped. Business/brand edits or expired opportunities invalidate approval/export until a new explicit workflow. Never publish automatically.
