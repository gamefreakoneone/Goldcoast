# 0020 Social creatives and testimonial quotes

New campaigns create one 1080x1440 Instagram post and optional 1080x1920 Story image. Types are auto, product, timely, comic and testimonial. A comic is one 2x2 image with typed four-panel script and compositor-rendered dialogue. Uploaded testimonial videos produce timestamped transcript and proposed exact quotes requiring manager review. Preserve historical landscape/portrait export. Product photos are selected by stable product identity. Every output has a judge verdict.
